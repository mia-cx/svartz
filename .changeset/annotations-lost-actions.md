Comments listed after the note, because their passage is gone, now have **+1** and **Delete** like a card. **Delete** is offered to the comment's author and to moderators, asks first, and locks while it's sent. The list's count drops as entries go, focus moves to the next entry, and the list goes with its last one. A signed-out reader's **+1** on a listed comment is applied when they come back from signing in.
