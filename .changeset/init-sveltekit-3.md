`svartz init` scaffolds a SvelteKit 3 project: Vite 8, `@sveltejs/vite-plugin-svelte` 7, `@sveltejs/adapter-static` 4 and TypeScript 6, with the SvelteKit config passed to `sveltekit()` in `vite.config.ts` instead of `svelte.config.js`. Existing SvelteKit 2 apps keep working as hosts.
