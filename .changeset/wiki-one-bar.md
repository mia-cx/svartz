The wiki header is one bar: the site name, the sections, then search and colour mode, 3.5rem tall where it used to take two rows. Sections that don't fit the bar's width wait in a **More** menu at its end, each opening its pages as a flyout. More carries the current section's mark while that section waits there. On phones and tablets the menu button and drawer work as before.
