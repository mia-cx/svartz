`theme.logo` shows a site's mark before its title in every theme's header. `src` is the mark; an optional `dark` replaces it in dark mode, following the reader's pinned colour mode or else the OS. Paths from the site root get the deployment base; URLs pass through. `SiteLogo` renders it for custom headers.
