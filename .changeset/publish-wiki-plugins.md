The community wiki plugins are on npm. `@svartz/plugin-submissions` turns readers' edits and new pages into commits or pull requests, `@svartz/plugin-history` gives every page a history with diffs and the wiki a Recent changes list, and `@svartz/plugin-moderation` lets readers report problems and moderators handle them from the dashboard. They ship in the fixed version group with the rest of Svartz.
