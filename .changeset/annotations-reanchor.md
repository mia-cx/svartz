Comments stay on their passage through light edits to the note: a fixed typo, an inserted word, a rewrapped sentence, or text added before it. A comment whose passage was deleted or rewritten is no longer dropped: it is listed after the note, with its author and the quote it responded to. A passage is only matched where at least 60% of its quote and surrounding text still agree, so a deleted passage never jumps to the same words elsewhere. See "Showing annotations on a page" in `docs/annotations.md`.
