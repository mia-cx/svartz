Obsidian has thirteen callout types, plus aliases. Unknown types render as notes.

> [!note]
> A note, with no custom title.

> [!abstract] Summary
> The station measures temperature, humidity, and pressure, and posts them every five minutes.

> [!info]
> Informational. Aliases: none.

> [!todo] Before winter
> - [ ] Add a heater to the dry box
> - [x] Order the rain gauge

> [!tip]
> Tips. Aliases: `hint`, `important`.

> [!success]
> It worked. Aliases: `check`, `done`.

> [!question]+ Foldable and open
> Aliases: `help`, `faq`. Click the title to fold this one.

> [!warning]
> Aliases: `caution`, `attention`.

> [!failure]- Foldable and closed
> Aliases: `fail`, `missing`. This text starts hidden.

> [!danger]
> Alias: `error`.

> [!bug]
> Something broke, with a `code span` inside.

> [!example]
> An example.

> [!quote] Luke Howard, 1803
> Clouds are subject to certain distinct modifications, produced by the general causes which effect all the variations of the atmosphere.

> [!faq] An alias
> `faq` renders as a question.

> [!recipe] A custom type
> Unknown types keep their title and fall back to the note style.

> [!note] Nested
> Callouts can hold other callouts.
