Two weeks, 4,032 readings, three gaps. All three gaps happened after 03:00 on cloudy nights, when the battery dipped below 3.3 V.

> [!question] Why only after 03:00?
> The battery drains overnight and the solar panel starts charging around 07:00. The gaps lined up with the lowest charge. A larger battery should close them.

> [!success] Pressure matches the airport
> After the altitude correction, sea-level pressure tracks the nearest airport's reports within 0.4 hPa.

Rain gauge parts arrive next week. Details in [[hardware/sensors#Pressure]] and the [Bosch BMP390 datasheet](https://www.bosch-sensortec.com/products/environmental-sensors/pressure-sensors/bmp390/).
