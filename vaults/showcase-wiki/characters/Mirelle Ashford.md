**Mirelle Ashford** is the Lamplighter of [[Vessa]] and the player's first character. She tends the lanterns of the Lower Wards and speaks for them at the council.

## Background

Mirelle grew up on the river barges that carry oil into the city.[^barges] She joined the Lamplighters' Guild at fourteen and became its youngest warden during the flood of the third winter.

## Gameplay

Mirelle's abilities make her strongest early in the night, when lanterns burn fastest.

> [!tip] Opening move
> Use *Quick fill* on the three lanterns nearest the harbour before the first tide. They are the hardest to reach later.

### Changes in 1.4

- *Trim wick* now doubles burn time instead of adding 50%.
- *Quick fill* no longer costs a turn when used at a guild post.
