The **Lamplighters' Guild** tends every public lantern in [[Vessa]]. Its wardens split the city into wards and answer to the council for each one that goes dark.

Playable lamplighters:

- [[Mirelle Ashford]], warden of the Lower Wards
- [[Tobin Reed]], her apprentice
