**Tobin Reed** is [[Mirelle Ashford]]'s apprentice. He can light lanterns but not refuel them, so he pairs well with a player who plans oil runs ahead.

## Gameplay

Tobin moves two districts per turn instead of one. His lanterns burn for half the normal time.
