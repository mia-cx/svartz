**Vessa** is a river city built on stilts above a delta that floods every winter. Its nine districts are joined by bridges that the tide closes twice a night.

## Districts

| District | Lanterns | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower Wards | 14 | Mirelle's home district |
| Harbour | 9 | Hardest to reach after the first tide |
| Guild Row | 6 | Refuel here without spending a turn |

## History

Vessa kept its lanterns lit through forty winters before the events of [[the-long-night|the Long Night]].
