**The Long Night** is Lanternfall's main mode. It runs from dusk to first light in twelve turns.

## Turn structure

1. The tide rises or falls, opening and closing bridges.
2. Each lantern burns one measure of oil.
3. Players move and act.
4. Districts with no lit lanterns go dark.

> [!warning] Dark districts
> A district that stays dark for two turns is lost for the rest of the night.

## Winning

You win if at least five districts are lit at first light. See [[oil-economy]] for how to keep enough oil in reserve.
