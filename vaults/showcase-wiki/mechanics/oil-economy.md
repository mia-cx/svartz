Lantern oil is the only resource in Lanternfall. The river guilds sell it at posts along Guild Row in [[Vessa]].

## Prices

Oil costs one coin per measure at dusk and rises by one coin every three turns.[^prices]

## Storage

Each keeper carries four measures. [[Mirelle Ashford]]'s *Quick fill* draws from her own supply.

[^prices]: Prices were halved on the Easy setting in update 1.2.
