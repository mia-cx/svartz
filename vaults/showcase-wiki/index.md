Welcome to the **Lanternfall Wiki**, the reference for [[the-long-night|the Long Night]], its keepers, and the city of [[Vessa]]. Anyone can read it; editors keep it in an Obsidian vault.

Lanternfall is a slow strategy game. You tend lanterns across a flooded city, trade oil with the river guilds, and decide which districts go dark when supplies run short.

## Start here

- New players: read [[the-long-night|The Long Night]] for the rules of the night, then [[oil-economy|the oil economy]].
- Returning players: the [[Mirelle Ashford]] page covers the 1.4 changes to the Lamplighter.
