Rotation keeps soil healthy and pests guessing. With four beds, each crop family returns to the same soil only every fourth year.

| Bed | Year 1 | Year 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Potatoes | Legumes |
| B | Legumes | Brassicas |
| C | Brassicas | Roots |
| D | Roots | Potatoes |

The table repeats: shift every crop one bed down each spring.
