I took on plot 14 last September with a spade, a borrowed fork, and more enthusiasm than planning. A year later the plot feeds us from June to October, and I know which of my early decisions were right.

## Start with the soil, not the seeds

The previous tenant had grown potatoes in the same beds for years. My first carrots came up forked and small. A soil test cost less than a packet of seed and would have told me to rest two beds and dig in compost first.

## Grow what you eat

I planted kohlrabi because the seed packet looked interesting. Nobody in our house eats kohlrabi. Next year the space goes to more beans.

> [!tip] The one tool
> A long-handled hoe, used for ten minutes twice a week, kept the weeds down better than any afternoon of hand-weeding.
