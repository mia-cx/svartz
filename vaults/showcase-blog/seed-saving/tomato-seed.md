---
title: Fermenting tomato seed
excerpt: Why tomato seed needs a few days in a jar before it dries.
author: Mia
published_at: 2026-09-18
cover: cover-9.svg
tags: [seed-saving, tomatoes]
---

Tomato seed sits in a gel that stops it germinating. Scoop the seeds into a jar with a little water, leave them three days until a film forms, then rinse and dry.
